01 / About Sahat

The person behind the questions

Sahat Kasa is a podcast host drawn to the stories and ideas that shape how we see Nepal and the world. Through open conversations, he brings together people with different experiences and gives them room to explain what they believe, what they have built and what they have learned.

02 / About the podcast

Why The SJK Podcast exists

The SJK Podcast is a place to slow down and look closer. One episode might explore politics and power; the next might follow an artist’s creative journey or the decisions behind a business. The goal is to leave each conversation with a clearer question, a new perspective or something worth discussing further.

Listen closely.Question clearly.Leave with perspective.

Before BBC World Questions Nepal

Ahead of BBC World Questions Nepal, Sahat Kasa sat down with BBC correspondent Jonny Dymond on The SJK Podcast. Their conversation explored why Nepal matters in global discussions, its changing politics and the voices shaping its future. The interview was also the subject of an article published by Los Angeles Vantage.
